# Ethics Policy

## Ethical Use of AgentOS

AgentOS is designed for ethical, defensive, and educational cybersecurity purposes only.

## Core Principles

1. **Defensive First**: All tools prioritize defensive security operations
2. **Education & Research**: Tools support security education and research
3. **Compliance**: Tools help organizations meet security compliance requirements
4. **Privacy**: Tools respect privacy and data protection regulations
5. **Transparency**: All operations include safety_metadata for auditability

## Prohibited Uses

AgentOS must NOT be used for:

- Unauthorized access to systems
- Exploitation of vulnerabilities
- Creation or distribution of malware
- Password cracking or brute-force attacks
- Active network scanning without authorization
- Any illegal or unethical activities

## Responsible Disclosure

If you discover vulnerabilities in AgentOS itself, please report them responsibly through appropriate channels.

## Compliance & Legal

Users are responsible for ensuring their use of AgentOS complies with:
- Local and international laws
- Organizational policies
- Industry regulations
- Ethical guidelines

## Governance

The Governance Engine enforces these ethical principles automatically. All operations are logged and auditable.
